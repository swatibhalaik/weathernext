=== Known input-only variables (forcings) -- never appear in predictions.nc ===
These condition the model as known future inputs; they are not forecast targets,
so their absence from predictions.nc is expected, not a gap:
  - toa_incident_solar_radiation (present in source dataset: False): Top-of-atmosphere incident solar radiation. A genuine physical driver of weather (day/night, season) that the model is TOLD as a known future input at every lead time (it's fully predictable from orbital mechanics alone) rather than something it has to forecast. Not a prediction target.
  - year_progress_sin (present in source dataset: True): sin(2*pi*fraction of year elapsed) -- an encoding of calendar season, given to the model as context, not predicted.
  - year_progress_cos (present in source dataset: True): cos() counterpart of the above, together giving the model an unambiguous point on the yearly cycle.
  - day_progress_sin (present in source dataset: True): sin(2*pi*fraction of day elapsed) -- time-of-day encoding (for the diurnal cycle), given as context, not predicted.
  - day_progress_cos (present in source dataset: True): cos() counterpart of the above.

=== Known static input variables -- also never appear in predictions.nc ===
  - geopotential_at_surface (present in source dataset: True): Static surface elevation (as geopotential) -- terrain doesn't change during a 15-day forecast, so it's given once as a fixed map, not predicted.
  - land_sea_mask (present in source dataset: True): Static land/sea/ice classification per grid cell -- also fixed, given as context (e.g. so the model can treat ocean and land surface temperature differently), not predicted.
